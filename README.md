## Project Objective

Analyze agricultural activities across Kharif, Rabi and Zaid seasons and identify meaningful patterns, trends, relationships and differences in agricultural performance.

## Analysis Covered

- Dataset exploration and data quality
- Missing values and duplicate checks
- Seasonal yield and production
- Revenue, cost and profit
- Rainfall and environmental conditions
- Water efficiency
- Crop-wise seasonal comparison
- Irrigation analysis
- Disease/pest risk
- Correlation with profit
- Findings, recommendations and conclusion
